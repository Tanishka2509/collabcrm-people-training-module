Employee Side — Prototype Updates

CollabCRM Training Portal
KPI & Look-up Filter Tabs & Breadcrumbs Modules-First & Assessment Gate Auto-Complete & Recognition

1. My Training — KPI Filtering & Look-up Filter

Teal
  • Clicking any KPI card (Total Assigned, Not Started, In Progress, Overdue, Completed) filters the table below; click again to reset.
  • Full-width Look-up Filter (Σ ⌄) & Quick Search (🔍 ⌄) bar above the table.
  • Fields: Training Name, Category, Assigned By, Status, Assigned Date, Due Date with Contains / Is operators, live suggestions, and + multi-filter pills.

2. Course Tabs, Labels & Breadcrumb Navigation

Indigo
  • Removed tab numbers: Overview, Learning Resources, Assessment & Questions.
  • Removed Strictly from passing criteria and redundant type labels.
  • Removed ← Back to Assessments button; top breadcrumbs (Training › Course Name › Assessment) handle back navigation.

3. Modules-First Flow & Go for Assessment

Orange
  • Assessment & Questions tab stays disabled (no lock icon) until all learning modules are completed.
  • Modules unlock sequentially: Mark Done → Completed ✓ (remaining modules show Pending).
  • No auto-redirect after the final Mark Done; instead, a right-aligned Go for Assessment → button appears below Learning Resources.
Complete Modules → Click "Go for Assessment →" → Score ≥ 80% → Completed + Certificate & Skills

4. Auto-Completion (≥ 80%) & Recognition

Violet
  • Passing the assessment (≥ passingScore, default 80%) automatically marks the training Completed (100%).
  • Linked Certificate and Skills are automatically awarded to the employee's profile, Skill Matrix, and Certificates.
