Admin / HR / RM Side — Prototype Updates

CollabCRM People & Training
Look-up Filter & Quick Search Training Progress (Employee & Training Views) Training Management & Recognition Certificates & Master Data

1. Look-up Filter & Quick Search Across All Tables

Teal
  • Full-width Look-up Filter (Σ ⌄) & Quick Search (🔍 ⌄) bar placed in a dedicated row directly above every table with Contains / Is operators, live suggestions, and + multi-filter pills.
  • Employees: Name, Code, Business Unit, Department, Designation, Reporting To, Email, Email Type.
  • Training Management: Training Name, Category, Department, Difficulty Level, Status, Availability.
  • Training Progress: Employee-wise (Employee Name, Department, Assigned Training, Training Status, Start Date, Due Date) & Training-wise (Training Name, Category, Difficulty Level, Status).
  • Certificates: Training Name, Employee Name, Department, Certificate Status, Issue Date, Training Category.

2. Training Progress — Side Panel & Pending Column

Indigo
  • Employee-wise Side Panel: Clicking an employee row opens a structured side drawer showing Start Date, Due Date, Modules Completed, Training Progress, and a Module Progress breakdown table.
  • Training-wise Pending Column: Added Pending count column alongside Enrolled, Completed, In Progress, Overdue, Avg Progress, and Status.

3. Training Management & Recognition Config

Orange
  • Training Recognition: Configure Certificate issuance and Skill Matrix awards (with Skill Level) directly on training programmes; dropdown chevrons and selection pills aligned cleanly.
  • Role-Based Availability & Safeguards: Active / Inactive availability toggles restricted to Admin / HR / RM roles; blocks deactivating trainings already started by employees.

4. Certificates & Master Data Administration

Violet
  • Certificates: Grouped by training with expandable employee certificate records, status badges, issue dates, and certificate preview modal.
  • Skills & Categories: Admin CRUD with case-insensitive duplicate checks and safeguards preventing deletion/deactivation of categories or skills assigned to active trainings.
